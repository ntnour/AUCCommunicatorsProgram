Your project, step by step

The project is how we measure your growth. Choose something you will do anyway, such as an upcoming event, a content series that raises awareness, a push to gain followers, or the launch of a new program.

  1. Pick your project

    Choose an event, a content task, or a launch from your own work.

  2. Set your KPIs

    Agree on measurable goals with Nourhan and your advisor or supervisor.

  3. Submit by Oct 26

    Send it through the form that matches your role (see below).

  4. Evaluation

    At the end of spring we review the KPIs you reached.

Upcoming workshop themes

These themes will run across the fall and spring semesters. Dates will be announced as they are confirmed.

Data & Analytics Basics

  • How to read Instagram, TikTok, and email analytics without getting overwhelmed
  • The five numbers every student communicator should track
  • Turning data into decisions: what to do more of, and what to stop
  • Simple reporting templates to share results with your team or leadership

Social Media Best Practices

  • Platform-specific strategy: Instagram vs. TikTok vs. email vs. WhatsApp
  • What the algorithm actually rewards in 2026, and how to work with it, not against it
  • Content calendars: how to plan a month of posts without burning out
  • Engagement tactics that build a real community, not just follower counts

Building a User Journey Across Offices & Clubs

  • What a user journey is and why it matters for student engagement
  • Mapping the touchpoints: from awareness to interest to attendance to community
  • Coordinating across clubs and offices to avoid communication conflicts and duplication
  • Practical exercise: map your org's current user journey and identify the gaps

AI Tools for Content Production

  • How to use AI software (ChatGPT, Claude, Canva AI) to produce social media content
  • Creating event campaigns from a brief, using AI to accelerate production
  • Prompting techniques for captions, graphics briefs, and email subject lines
  • What AI can do for you, and where human judgment still has to lead

The AUC Communications Landscape

  • Overview of all Student Life Area offices and their communication channels
  • How communications flows between DOS, MARCOM, and student organizations
  • What "representing AUC" actually means in your content and messaging
  • Understanding the difference between institutional channels and org-owned channels

Team Leadership, Campaign Strategy & Workflow Mastery

  • Personal & team organization: master time management techniques and establish structured content calendars for seamless campaign rollouts.
  • Maximizing resources: leverage available tools, assets, and team strengths for maximum impact.
  • Effective briefing: craft precise, actionable briefs for designers and collaborators that minimize revisions and get results.
  • Workflow best practices: build healthy communication channels and delegation habits that keep projects moving from kickoff to launch.
